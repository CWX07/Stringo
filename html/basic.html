Strings

“Know the strings, know the sound.”

01

A standard guitar has 6 strings. Each strings create different musical notes.

02

String names (Standard Tuning):
E - A - D - G - B - E.

03

Use the mnemonic:
“Eat All Day Get Big Energy”
to remember string order.

04

Each string can be played open (no frets) or pressed on a fret to get different notes.

Fretboards

“Understand every part of the guitar and how it creates sound.”

swipe
01

Frets are the thin metal strips on the guitar neck that divide it into sections.

02

Each fret raises the pitch of a string by one semitone when pressed down.

03

Most guitars have 19 to 24 frets. The higher the fret number, the higher the note.

04

Fret markers (dots) on the 3rd, 5th, 7th, 9th, and 12th frets help you navigate quickly.
